# Padding Oracle: Research Log

This entry records the completion of a Dreamhack padding-oracle challenge. I have intentionally omitted a detailed solution because the platform limits the publication of challenge answers. The useful result is therefore the direction of study rather than the exploit procedure itself.

My recent work has concentrated on cryptography and reverse engineering. Anti-debugging tasks remain a recurring obstacle: repeated study of introductory reversing has improved static-analysis speed, but it has not yet produced a systematic method for reasoning about anti-analysis techniques.

For April, I established two measurable objectives:

1. enter the top 50 on Dreamhack by completing a broad set of lower-level challenges; and
2. supplement challenge solving with focused case studies when a technique recurs.

I also intend to publish subsequent write-ups in English. Writing in a second language introduces additional editing cost, but it makes the technical record accessible to a wider audience and forces ambiguous reasoning to be stated more explicitly.

The immediate conclusion is straightforward: consistent accumulation of small, verified results is more valuable than waiting for a single advanced solution. Team participation may become useful later, but improving the underlying analysis process remains the current priority.
